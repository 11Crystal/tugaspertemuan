Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services, processes, and international trade solutions.

🔍

🛠️ Services & Process

We provide comprehensive international trade services including global sourcing, logistics and shipping, quality assurance, trade finance, consultation services, and customs clearance. Our end-to-end solutions cover every aspect of your import needs from supplier identification to final delivery.
Our sourcing process begins with understanding your requirements, followed by identifying and vetting suitable suppliers from our global network. We conduct due diligence, negotiate terms, arrange samples, and facilitate communication throughout the entire sourcing journey.
Yes, we provide complete customs clearance services and handle all import documentation including commercial invoices, packing lists, bills of lading, certificates of origin, and any required permits or licenses. Our experienced team ensures compliance with all regulations.
Absolutely! We work with manufacturers who can customize products according to your specifications, including private labeling, custom packaging, color variations, and feature modifications. We'll coordinate the entire customization process for you.

💰 Pricing & Payment

Our pricing structure is transparent and competitive. We typically charge a service fee based on the order value, plus actual costs for logistics, quality inspection, and any additional services. No hidden fees - everything is clearly outlined in your quote.
Payment terms vary depending on the service and order size. For new clients, we typically require a deposit to begin work, with the balance due at agreed milestones. We accept various payment methods including wire transfer, letters of credit, and trade financing options.
Minimum order requirements depend on the product type and supplier. While we work with orders of various sizes, some manufacturers have minimum quantities. We'll help you find suppliers that match your volume requirements and budget.
Yes, we offer various trade financing solutions including letters of credit, trade credit insurance, and flexible payment terms. Our finance team can help structure deals that work within your cash flow requirements.

🚚 Shipping & Logistics

We offer multiple shipping options including sea freight (FCL and LCL), air freight, express courier, and multimodal transportation. We'll recommend the most cost-effective and time-efficient method based on your cargo type, urgency, and budget.
Shipping times vary by origin, destination, and method. Sea freight typically takes 15-45 days, air freight 3-7 days, and express courier 1-3 days. We provide detailed transit time estimates for each specific route and shipping option.
Yes, we offer comprehensive cargo insurance to protect your goods during transit. Insurance coverage includes protection against damage, loss, theft, and other risks. We recommend appropriate coverage levels based on your cargo value and risk profile.
Absolutely! We provide real-time tracking information and regular updates throughout the shipping process. You'll receive tracking numbers and access to our online portal where you can monitor your shipment's progress from departure to delivery.

✅ Quality Assurance

We implement a comprehensive quality assurance program including supplier audits, pre-production samples, in-line inspections, and final quality control checks. Our QA team conducts thorough inspections based on your specifications and international standards.
Yes, we provide detailed inspection reports with photos, measurements, and test results. Reports cover appearance, functionality, packaging, labeling, and compliance with your specifications. You'll receive these reports before shipment for approval.
If products don't meet agreed specifications, we work with suppliers to resolve issues through rework, replacement, or negotiated solutions. Our quality guarantee ensures you receive products that meet your requirements or we'll make it right.
Yes, we coordinate third-party testing and certifications through accredited laboratories. This includes safety testing, compliance certifications, material analysis, and any industry-specific testing required for your market.

👤 Account & Support

Getting started is easy! Simply request a quote through our website or contact our team directly. We'll schedule a consultation to understand your needs and provide a customized solution. No upfront commitments required for initial discussions.
To provide an accurate quote, we need product specifications, quantity requirements, target pricing, delivery destination, timeline, and any special requirements. The more details you provide, the more accurate and comprehensive our quote will be.
Yes! We work with businesses of all sizes, from startups to large corporations. Our scalable services and flexible approach make international trade accessible for small businesses looking to source products globally or expand their supply chain.
Our support continues beyond delivery. We provide ongoing account management, help with any delivery issues, assist with warranty claims, facilitate reorders, and offer market insights for future sourcing decisions. We're your long-term partner in international trade.
Our support team is available 24/7 through multiple channels: phone (+1-555-TRADE-01), email (support@worldwidetrade.com), live chat on our website, or through your dedicated account manager. We typically respond within 2 hours during business hours.

We serve diverse industries including electronics, textiles and apparel, machinery, chemicals, automotive, medical devices, consumer goods, and more. Our experienced team has expertise across multiple sectors and can adapt to specific industry requirements.
Our rigorous vetting process includes business license verification, facility inspections, quality system audits, financial stability checks, reference validation, and ongoing performance monitoring. We only work with suppliers who meet our strict standards.
Yes, we assist with regulatory compliance including FDA registrations, CE marking, FCC certifications, and other market-specific requirements. Our compliance team stays updated on regulations and helps ensure your products meet all necessary standards.
Our comprehensive approach combines deep industry expertise, advanced technology, rigorous quality control, transparent pricing, and personalized service. We're not just a service provider – we're your strategic partner in international trade success.
