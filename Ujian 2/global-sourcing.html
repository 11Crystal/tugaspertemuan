Our Sourcing Process

A systematic approach to finding the right suppliers and products for your business needs.

1

Requirements Analysis

We analyze your product specifications, quality requirements, budget constraints, and delivery timeline to create a comprehensive sourcing brief.

  • Product specification review
  • Quality standard definition
  • Budget and volume assessment
  • Timeline planning
2

Supplier Identification

Using our extensive database and network, we identify potential suppliers that match your requirements and have the capability to deliver.

  • Database search and filtering
  • Capability matching
  • Geographic optimization
  • Preliminary screening
3

Supplier Evaluation

Comprehensive evaluation of shortlisted suppliers including factory visits, capability assessments, and financial health checks.

  • Factory audits and visits
  • Production capability assessment
  • Quality system evaluation
  • Financial stability check
4

Quotation & Negotiation

Request for quotations from qualified suppliers and negotiate terms to secure the best possible deal for your business.

  • RFQ preparation and distribution
  • Quote analysis and comparison
  • Price negotiation
  • Terms and conditions finalization
5

Sample Development

Coordinate sample production and evaluation to ensure the product meets your specifications before placing bulk orders.

  • Sample request coordination
  • Sample evaluation and feedback
  • Specification refinement
  • Final approval process
6

Supplier Selection

Final supplier selection based on comprehensive evaluation results and your specific business requirements.

  • Comparative analysis
  • Risk assessment
  • Final recommendation
  • Contract finalization
