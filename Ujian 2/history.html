Our Journey Through Time

From a small startup in Medan to a global trade leader serving clients worldwide - discover our remarkable growth story.

Company Timeline

🚀

2015 - Foundation

Founded in Medan, North Sumatra by a team of trade professionals with a vision to simplify global sourcing for Indonesian businesses.

🌱

2016 - First Growth

Established partnerships with 50+ suppliers across Asia and served our first 100 clients, focusing on electronics and textiles.

🌏

2017 - Regional Expansion

Expanded operations to Malaysia and Singapore, opening our first international office and growing our supplier network to 200+.

💻

2018 - Digital Platform

Launched our proprietary digital platform for order tracking, supplier management, and real-time communication with clients.

🏆

2019 - Recognition

Received "Best Emerging Trade Company" award and achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification for quality management systems.

🌍

2020 - Global Reach

Despite pandemic challenges, expanded to serve clients in 25+ countries and launched our quality assurance division.

📈

2021 - Major Growth

Reached 5,000+ clients milestone, established Shanghai office, and launched trade finance solutions for SMEs.

🔮

2022 - Innovation

Introduced AI-powered supplier matching, blockchain-based documentation, and expanded into machinery and chemicals sectors.

🎯

2023 - Excellence

Achieved 10,000+ client milestone, won "Best Trade Solutions Provider 2023" award, and launched sustainability initiatives.

🌟

2024 - Market Leader

Became the leading trade solutions provider in Southeast Asia with 500+ verified suppliers and 15,000+ successful transactions.

🚀

2025 - Future Vision

Expanding into new markets, launching advanced analytics platform, and targeting 50,000+ clients by the end of 2025.
